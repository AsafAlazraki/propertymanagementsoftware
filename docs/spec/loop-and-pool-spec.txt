1. Executive Justification: Solving the Reluctant Investor's Dilemma

Across New Zealand and Australia, everyday Kiwi and Australian property investors (owning 1 to 4
properties) face an unfair binary choice: either surrender 7.5% to 10% + GST of gross rental income
($2,700 to $4,500 per property annually) to traditional property management agencies, or manage
the tenancy themselves using spreadsheets and basic DIY form tools, creating a stressful second job
and exposing themselves to severe statutory penalties.

Traditional DIY platforms (such as myRent in New Zealand, with roughly 55,000 landlords) only solve
basic record-keeping and force landlords and tenants into a single generic interface. When a pipe
bursts at 2:00 AM, a routine inspection falls due while the owner is out of town, or a tenant wants a
portable renter profile across homes, both sides are left unsupported.

  Core Value Proposition: "Autopilot Software + On-Demand Physical Legs + Two-Sided
  Network"
  90% of property management is software-automatable (rent reconciliation, arrears chasing,
  statutory notice timing, Healthy Homes compliance, bond lodgements, and tax categorisation).
  The remaining 10% (physical inspections, open home viewings, key handovers, and
  Tenancy Tribunal representation) is decoupled into an on-demand marketplace fulfilled by
  vetted local inspectors and partner agencies. This saves investors $2,500+ per year while
  allowing them to manage every physical task remotely from their phone.

1.2 Australasian Expansion Strategy (New Zealand First, Australia Second)

New Zealand serves as the ideal launchpad due to a single national Residential Tenancies Act (RTA),
high residential investment concentration, and modern digital government infrastructure (Tenancy
Services Online Bond Hub). Once proven in Nelson, Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch, the
modular jurisdiction rules engine expands into Australia (NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA) by swapping state
legislative rule packs without rewriting the core product.

2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

The following SOPs govern daily platform operations so landlords, tenants, tradespeople, and
administrators understand exact workflows clearly.

SOP 1: Dual-Track Landlord Onboarding (First-Time Rental vs. Existing Tenancy)

1. Path A: Renting a Property for the First Time (New-to-Market Wizard):
     Step 1 (Address & Ownership Setup): Landlord enters the property address in Loop (auto-
     populating council/title data) and selects ownership structure (Individual, Family Trust, LTC, or
     Joint Partnership).
     Step 2 (Pre-Letting Compliance Check): Guided 5-point Healthy Homes calculator (heating
     kW size, ceiling/underfloor insulation, extractor fans, ground moisture barrier, draught
     stopping), smoke alarm verification, and Body Corporate rules upload (if Unit Title).
     Step 3 (Key Custody & Marketing): Landlord registers spare key access via a City Key
     Collection Depot (KeyNest NZ) or on-site lockbox, sets a fixed weekly rent (enforcing RTA s
     22F), and publishes directly to Pool Search, Trade Me, and realestate.co.nz.

2. Path B: Switching an Existing Tenancy ("30-Second AI Magic Import"):
     Step 1 (Document Upload): Landlord uploads their current PDF tenancy agreement and an
     optional CSV bank statement export.
     Step 2 (AI Reconstruction): AI extracts tenant details, weekly rent, bond lodgement ID,
     chattels, and Healthy Homes dates, building the live ledger in 30 seconds.
     Step 3 (Autopilot & Tenant Link): Landlord links Open Banking, sets their auto-approve
     repair threshold, and invites the tenant to connect their Pool account.

SOP 2: Automated Rent Reconciliation, Sinking Funds & Arrears Escalation

1. Daily Bank Matching: Incoming rent payments are matched automatically via Open Banking
  references and logged into the IR3R tax-categorised ledger.

2. Maintenance Sinking Fund Skim: On every cleared rent payment, the configured percentage
  (e.g., 5% to 8%) is virtually ring-fenced inside the property's Maintenance Vault toward
  forecasted CapEx replacements (hot water cylinder, heat pump, roof).

3. Tiered Arrears & Personal Call Protocol:
     Day 1 Late (Friendly Check-In): Platform sends a polite SMS/email reminder to the tenant and
     prompts the landlord: "Call Tenant Directly" (one-tap dial with script) or "Have Loop Call &
     Report Back" (a Loop support concierge phones the tenant to check if it was an accidental
     bank delay and logs feedback in the ledger).

     Day 5 Late: Formal RTA-compliant arrears notice drafted and dispatched; landlord notified.

     Day 14 / Day 21 Late: System generates a 14-Day Notice to Remedy or prepares a Tenancy
     Tribunal termination and debt enforcement pack for one-click approval.

SOP 3: Maintenance Triage, Quotes, Tenant-Fault Verification & Key Release

1. Tenant Self-Help First (via Pool): Inside their Pool app, the tenant sees visual 60-second
  troubleshooting cards (checking RCD safety switches, clearing sink hair traps, resetting heat
  pump remotes), resolving roughly 25% of minor issues immediately.

2. AI Urgency Classification & Fault Screening: If unresolved, the tenant submits photos/video. AI
  classifies urgency (Emergency RTA s 45, Urgent, or Routine) and flags potential tenant-caused
  damage (e.g., broken window, blocked drain from foreign objects, lost keys).

3. Quote-First vs. Immediate Auto-Dispatch:
     Urgent / Within Pre-Approved Limit: Dispatches a vetted local tradesperson immediately up to
     the landlord's Sinking Fund limit (e.g., $350).

     Non-Urgent or Over Limit (Quote Mode): Tradesperson receives a magic link to inspect and
     upload an itemised GST quote first. The landlord reviews and approves the quote in one tap
     before work begins.

4. Gated Key Access: A time-restricted key depot code or lockbox PIN is released to the
  tradesperson only after the statutory 24-hour entry notice window has cleared (or immediately for
  genuine emergencies).

5. Invoice Settlement & Tenant-Fault Liability Verification: When uploading the final GST invoice
  and completion photos, the tradesperson indicates whether the root cause was Fair Wear & Tear /
  Building Fault or Tenant Damage / Misuse. If tenant-caused, Pool requires the tenant to review
  and verify the tradesperson's photo report before routing the invoice:
     If confirmed (or verified via RTA liability rules), the invoice is billed directly to the tenant's Pool
     ledger (with optional payment plan) rather than draining the landlord's Sinking Fund.

     If disputed, the ticket is flagged for landlord mediation or Tenancy Tribunal evidence logging.

SOP 4: Routine Inspections & On-Demand Physical Outsourcing

1. Statutory Notice Lock: The system schedules routine inspections no more than once every 4
  weeks and enforces a hard minimum of 48 hours' written notice (plus service buffers) before any
  inspection slot can be booked.

2. DIY vs. Delegate Choice: Seven days prior, the landlord receives a prompt: "Complete Inspection
  Yourself" (using the guided room-by-room mobile photo checklist) or "Delegate to Local Pro
  ($85)".

3. Offline-Capable Field Execution: Mobile inspection checklists run as an offline-first Progressive
  Web App (PWA) with local photo caching so inspectors never lose data in basements, concrete
  apartments, or rural Tasman lifestyle blocks with weak mobile reception. Once back online, photos
  sync automatically and trigger a key-return alert.

SOP 5: 1-Click Tenancy Variations & Tenant Relationship Care

1. Instant Digital Amendments: For flatmate swaps, pet requests, fixed-term extensions, or mutual
  clauses, the landlord selects the variation type in Loop. The platform blocks illegal contracting-out
  clauses (RTA s 137), enforces the 21-day pet response deadline and 2-week Pet Bond cap, and
  dispatches e-signature prompts (via native e-sign or DocuSign/Lumin) straight to the tenant's Pool
  app.

2. Retention & Home Care Nudges: Tenants receive winter condensation/mould prevention tips in
  Pool. At 6-month and 12-month milestones, landlords receive prompts in Loop to send a thank-
  you voucher alongside a Vacancy Cost Calculator before considering rent hikes.

SOP 6: Lifecycle Transitions (Prepare for Sale, Re-Tenanting on Pool, or Agency
Handover)

1. Prepare for Sale Mode: Generates a secure, shareable Vendor Due Diligence Data Room for
  real estate agents containing verified 12-month net yield, Healthy Homes and meth compliance
  certificates, bond IDs, and an automated 42-day vacant-possession notice calculator upon
  unconditional sale.

2. Re-Tenanting Autopilot (Loop to Pool Instant Syndication): Triggered automatically when
  periodic notice is given or within the 90-to-21-day fixed-term expiry window, orchestrating exit
  inspection, instant fixed-price listing syndication onto Pool Search, Trade Me, and
  realestate.co.nz, Privacy Act two-stage applicant vetting, and Online Bond Hub lodgement.

3. Partner Agency Handover / Raw Export: If an investor chooses to switch to a full-service
  agency or leave the platform, one click transfers the complete compliance and ledger bundle to an
  accredited partner PM (earning a $350 B2B referral fee) or downloads a clean ZIP/CSV archive
  with zero lock-in.

SOP 7: Pool Tenant Onboarding, Public Search & The Pool Rent Passport Score

1. Renter Profile Creation: Prospective tenants create a free Pool account, verify their identity
  once, and build a reusable, Privacy Act 2020-compliant renter profile.

2. The Pool Rent Passport Score (0 to 100 Verified Trust Rating): Tenants build a portable,
  objective reputation score calculated from three verified pillars:
     On-Time Rent Punctuality (50% weight): Percentage of rent payments cleared on or before due
     date across Pool-tracked tenancies.

     Inspection & Property Care Rating (30% weight): Average condition score across completed
     routine and exit inspections, plus zero unresolved tenant-fault maintenance debts.

     Tenancy Longevity & Verified References (20% weight): Completed lease terms and verified
     prior landlord/PM sign-offs.
