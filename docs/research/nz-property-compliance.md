### 1.1 Heating standard (HHS Regs subpart 2, Schedule 2)

| Requirement | Precise value / rule | Regulation or section | In force since | Source URL | Confidence | Notes, exemptions & edge cases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where heating is required | The **main living room** only (the only living room, or the largest if 2+). Must be heated by 1+ qualifying heaters with total capacity >= the required heating capacity | r 8(1)(a), r 8(2) | 1 Jul 2019 (r 8(1) replaced 12 May 2022) | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | "Living room" includes open-plan spaces; Tenancy Services tool guide says include any connected space that cannot be closed off by a door (kitchen, hallway, stairwell). | Qualifying heater | (a) installed as a fixture; (b) located in the living room, or supplies heat directly into it (duct/vent); (c) heating capacity **>= 1.5 kW**; (d) has a **thermostat** if it is an electric heater or fixed heat pump; (e) is not an "unacceptable heater" | r 9(1) | 1 Jul 2019 | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | Guidance: "fixed" = secured to the property; plug-in heaters can qualify if secured. | Unacceptable heaters | Open fire; unflued combustion heater (e.g. LPG cabinet heater); an electric heater (other than a fixed heat pump) where required heating capacity is **> 2.4 kW** | r 9(2) | 1 Jul 2019 | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | So plain electric panel heaters only work for rooms needing <= 2.4 kW (plus the top-up and shared-building exceptions below). |
| Target temperature | Required internal temperature **18 °C** on the coldest assumed day for the district | Sch 2 cl 3(1) (b = 18 °C), cl 4, cl 5 | 1 Jul 2019 | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | The standard is about heater *capacity*, not a measured room temperature. |
| Required heating capacity - standard formula | h (kW) = [t + v + (f x 40)] / 1000, where t = transmission heat loss (W), v = ventilation heat loss (W), f = living-room floor area (m2). t = [d + (e x g)] x (18 - c); v = volume (m3) x air-change rate x 0.34 x (18 - c). The "f x 40" term is a 40 W/m2 warm-up allowance. |
| Required heating capacity - **2022 "modern dwelling / relevant apartment" formula** | h (kW) = (t + v) x 1.2 / 1000, with g = 0.25 and air-change rate = 0.5. Results in smaller required heaters | Sch 2 cl 2(2), cl 3(1), cl 4; definitions r 3(1) | **12 May 2022** (SL 2022/109) | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | **Modern dwelling** = first building consent on/after 30 Sep 2008 (zone 1), 30 Jun 2008 (zone 2), 31 Oct 2007 (zone 3), OR earlier building where insulation and glazing throughout the thermal envelope meet/exceed NZS 4218:2009. **Relevant apartment** = premises in a building of 3+ storeys containing 6+ commercial or residential premises. If relied on, the compliance statement must say why (r 34(5)). |

| Legal status of the official calculator | A "heating capacity calculator" published by MBIE is presumed correct; a person using it in good faith "is entitled to rely on the result" | r 10(2)-(4) | 1 Jul 2019 | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | **Product implication:** only the official tool carries this presumption. | Alternative compliance pathway (specialist assessment) | A suitably qualified specialist assesses the capacity sufficient to heat the living room to at least 18 °C within 2 hours after an 8-hour period of disuse, and maintain 18 °C, using the Sch 2 external temperature | r 10A; "suitably qualified specialist" in r 3(1) | 12 May 2022 | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | Specialist = chartered professional engineer, IntPE registered with Engineering NZ, or NZQF level 7+ engineering/physics/building-science graduate with 5+ years heating system design. | Tolerance for heaters installed before 1 Jul 2019 | If the room is heated by "large heaters" (each a qualifying heater **> 2.4 kW**) installed before 1 Jul 2019 whose total capacity is **>= 80%** of the required capacity, the standard is met | Sch 1 cl 4 | 1 Jul 2019 | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | Must be declared in the compliance statement (cl 4(4)). | Evidence of compliance (heating) | Statement must give (a) the required heating capacity of the main living room and (b) each qualifying heater's type and capacity in kW | r 34(1) | Agreements signed on/after 1 Dec 2020 | [HHS Regs](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2019/88/en/latest/) | primary-verified | Keep: tool report, heater spec sheet/label photo, installer invoice, wood-burner consent. ## 12. Could not verify / conflicts found

1. **Climate zone boundaries** - The regulations define zones 1-3 only by reference to Appendix B of NZS 4218:2009 (a paid standard, not read). The Tenancy Services map shows zone 1 at the top of the North Island, zone 2 the rest of the North Island outside the central plateau, zone 3 the central plateau and South Island. My recollection is zone 1 = Northland, Auckland, Thames-Coromandel and zone 3 includes Taupo, Ruapehu and northern Rangitikei, but that is **unverified** - use the official insulation tool or the standard for district-level mapping.
2. **HUD vs regulation conflict** - hud.govt.nz lists the r 19A cut-off dates as zone 1 = 31 Oct 2007 and zone 3 = 30 September (no year). The regulation (and Tenancy Services guidance) says zone 1 = 30 Sep 2008, zone 2 = 30 Jun 2008, zone 3 = 31 Oct 2007. Follow the regulation.
3. **Penalty wording conflict** - Tenancy Services pages quote "up to $500" for a missing statement; the Act provides a $500 infringement fee (small landlords), $1,000 (6+ tenancies), and exemplary damages up to $750 ($900 if knowingly false). Both are correct for different enforcement routes.
4. **building.govt.nz guidance** (pool barrier technical details such as fence height, BWOF guidance, foil insulation ban notice, Building Code smoke alarm changes) - site blocked automated access; pool and BWOF rules above are from the Building Act itself. Building Code clause F9 details and any interconnected-alarm requirement are unverified.
5. **Ground moisture barrier polythene thickness** - set in NZS 4246:2016 section 8 (not read).
6. **Tower's inspection interval** and the full State and Initio policy wordings - not opened; State and Initio figures come from their own web pages.
7. **Asbestos regulations, Housing Improvement Regulations 1947, Unit Titles Act/Regulations, Electricity Act homeowner exemption** - not read directly; statements rely on WorkSafe, Tenancy Services and Unit Titles Services pages.
8. **Tenant penalty for tampering with smoke alarms ($4,000)** - the Act's $4,000 entry is for interfering with "means of escape from fire"; that this covers removing/disabling an alarm is Tenancy Services' statement (read via summary).
9. **Compliance statement on a mere variation** - s 13A(1CB) requires a statement on a written variation or renewal, while HHS r 33 mentions only agreements and renewals for the detailed information. Recommended conservative rule: full statement on all three.
10. **Summation formulas in Schedule 2** (values d and e) are rendered as images on the legislation site; "sum of area divided by R-value" is inferred from the variable definitions.

## Suggested compliance checklist

Ordered list of discrete checks to track **per property** (P) or **per tenancy** (T). "Evidence" = what should be uploaded/recorded to mark the check satisfied. Recurrences marked "(product default)" are recommendations, not legal intervals.

**A. Before letting / at property set-up**

1. **Lawful dwelling** (P) - Confirm the property can lawfully be lived in (consented, not an unconsented conversion). Recurs: once, and after any alteration. Evidence: code compliance certificate, council property file or LIM.
2. **Heating size calculated** (P) - Required kW for the main living room. Recurs: once; redo after layout, insulation or glazing changes. Evidence: official Heating Assessment Tool report (or specialist r 10A assessment with name and qualifications).
3. **Qualifying heater installed** (P) - Fixed heater(s) in the main living room, each >= 1.5 kW, thermostat if electric/heat pump, total >= required kW (or 80% tolerance / top-up rule recorded). Recurs: once; working-order check at every routine inspection. Evidence: heater model and kW (label photo or spec sheet), install date and invoice.
4. **Ceiling insulation** (P) - R 2.9 (zones 1-2) / R 3.3 (zone 3), or pre-Jul-2016 insulation >= 120 mm, in reasonable condition. Recurs: once; condition re-check yearly (product default) and after any roof-space work or leak. Evidence: installer certificate or product label, install date, thickness photos with a ruler, last-inspected date.
5. **Underfloor insulation** (P) - R 1.3 under all suspended floors, reasonable condition (no ripped/dull foil). Recurs: as item 4. Evidence: as item 4.
6. **Opening windows/doors** (P) - Every bedroom, living/dining room and kitchen has openable area >= 5% of floor area that can be fixed open. Recurs: once; re-check after alterations. Evidence: per-room measurements and photos.
7. **Kitchen extractor fan** (P) - Vents outdoors; 150 mm or 50 L/s if installed on/after 1 Jul 2019. Recurs: once; working check at every routine inspection. Evidence: fan and duct diameter or flow rate in writing, install date.
8. **Bathroom extractor fan(s)** (P) - Vents outdoors; 120 mm or 25 L/s if installed on/after 1 Jul 2019. Recurs: as item 7. Evidence: as item 7.
9. **Gutters, downpipes and drains** (P) - Present, intact, clear and draining to an outfall. Recurs: every routine inspection; clean at least yearly (product default). Evidence: dated photos, cleaning invoice.
10. **Ground moisture barrier** (P) - Needed if subfloor is enclosed (>= 50% of perimeter obstructed). Recurs: once; condition check yearly (product default). Evidence: photos, install invoice, or recorded "no enclosed subfloor".
11. **Draught stopping** (P) - No unreasonable gaps (3 mm / $2-coin rule of thumb); open fireplace blocked. Recurs: every routine inspection and at each new tenancy. Evidence: checklist with photos; if fireplace left usable, the tenant's written request and landlord's written agreement.
12. **Exemptions register** (P/T) - Each exemption relied on, with the regulation number, reason, evidence and expiry/review date. Recurs: review at each new tenancy and before any 12-month expiry. Evidence: installer letter, consent application, compliance document, body corporate correspondence.
13. **Smoke alarms** (P) - One within 3 m of each sleeping-space entrance (or in the room), one per level, photoelectric with 8-year+ battery or hard-wired, none past replacement date. Recurs: test at the start of every tenancy and at every routine inspection; replace by the printed replacement date. Evidence: per-alarm location, type, expiry date, photo, test log.
14. **Electrical safety** (P) - Fittings and supplied appliances are electrically safe; safe-use instructions given. Recurs: at the start of each tenancy (product default). Evidence: electrician report or certificate where work was done; appliance manuals issued to tenant.
15. **Gas safety** (P, if gas) - Appliances and installation not unsafe; safe-use instructions given. Recurs: at the start of each tenancy (NZS 5255 check is the legal safe harbour). Evidence: gasfitter's NZS 5255 safety check, gas certificates.
16. **Locks and security** (P) - All external doors and accessible windows reasonably secure. Recurs: start of each tenancy and on report. Evidence: inspection note, key register.
17. **Water supply** (P, if no town supply) - Adequate collection and storage; tank full at start. Recurs: start of each tenancy. Evidence: tank capacity record, fill receipt.
18. **Pool barrier** (P, if pool/spa >= 400 mm deep) - Compliant barrier; council notified. Recurs: council/IQPI inspection every 3 years; gate-latch check at every routine inspection. Evidence: certificate of periodic inspection with next-due date.
19. **Meth status** (P) - No known contamination above 15 µg/100 cm²; any Police/council notice or valid screening result triggers a detailed test as soon as practicable. Recurs: event-driven; optional baseline test between tenancies (required by some insurers). Evidence: lab report; post-decontamination clearance result <= 15.
20. **Insurance in force** (P) - Landlord policy current, insurer's conditions known (inspection interval, tenant vetting, arrears steps). Recurs: yearly at renewal. Evidence: policy schedule showing excesses and expiry.
21. **Building WOF** (P, multi-unit/boarding house with specified systems) - Current BWOF displayed. Recurs: yearly. Evidence: copy of BWOF (from body corporate if applicable).

**B. At each new tenancy, renewal or written variation**

22. **Written agreement** (T) - In writing with all s 13A(1) details, signed by landlord, copy given to tenant before the tenancy starts. Recurs: each agreement. Evidence: signed agreement with delivery timestamp.
23. **Healthy homes compliance statement** (T) - Signed statement of intent plus the detailed information for all five standards and any exemptions. Recurs: each new agreement, renewal and written variation. Evidence: signed statement.
24. **Insulation statement** (T) - Whether and where insulation exists (ceiling, floor, walls), type and condition, or the "could not obtain" explanation. Recurs: each new agreement. Evidence: signed statement (may be combined with item 23).
25. **Insurance statement** (T) - Insured or not; each relevant excess; policy available on request. Recurs: each new agreement; update the tenant in writing whenever cover or excess changes. Evidence: statement plus change notices.
26. **Body corporate rules attached** (T, unit titles) - Current operational rules set out with the agreement. Recurs: each new agreement; notify variations promptly. Evidence: rules version/date and proof of service.
27. **Entry condition report** (T) - Initial inspection with photos (also supports insurer conditions). Recurs: start and end of each tenancy. Evidence: signed property inspection report and photos.

**C. During the tenancy**

28. **Routine inspections** (T) - At the insurer's interval (3-monthly or 6-monthly), never more than once per 4 weeks, with 48 hours-14 days' notice. Recurs: per policy. Evidence: notice given, written report, photos.
29. **Repair requests** (T) - Log, acknowledge and resolve; urgent items as soon as reasonably possible. Recurs: event-driven. Evidence: timestamped log, contractor invoices, tenant updates.
30. **HHS items kept working** (P) - Heater, fans, insulation, barrier, seals maintained or replaced within a reasonable time. Recurs: checked at each routine inspection. Evidence: inspection checklist, service invoices.
31. **Heat pump / ducted system service** (P) - Per manufacturer (product default: yearly). Evidence: service invoice.
32. **Chimney / flue sweep** (P, if usable fire or wood burner) - Product default: yearly (insurer may require). Evidence: sweep certificate.
33. **Tenant HHS information request** (T) - Respond within 21 days. Recurs: event-driven. Evidence: request and response timestamps.
34. **Meth test results shared** (T) - Within 7 days of receipt. Recurs: event-driven. Evidence: written notice with report attached.
35. **Fibre request** (T) - Respond within 21 days. Recurs: event-driven. Evidence: written response.
36. **Asbestos check before works** (P) - Before renovation, refurbishment or demolition in a pre-2000 structure: identify asbestos and prepare a management plan for the affected area. Recurs: event-driven. Evidence: asbestos survey, management plan, removalist records.
37. **Licensed trades** (P) - Electrical, gas, plumbing, drainlaying and restricted building work done by licensed practitioners. Recurs: each job. Evidence: licence number, certificates of compliance.
38. **Overseas absence** (landlord) - Appoint an NZ agent if away more than 21 consecutive days. Recurs: event-driven. Evidence: agent details notified to tenant and Tenancy Services.
39. **MBIE document request** (T) - Produce within 10 working days. Recurs: event-driven. Evidence: response timestamp.

**D. After the tenancy**

40. **Records retained** (T) - Keep all s 123A documents for at least 12 months after termination (longer where other rules require). Recurs: once per tenancy. Evidence: archive with a retention-until date.

## Recent & upcoming changes

**Already in force (2025-2026)**

- **30 Jan, 20 Mar and 1 Dec 2025** - Residential Tenancies Amendment Act 2024 phases (terminations, technical changes, pets). Mostly outside this file's scope; relevant here are uncapped tenant liability for pet damage and enforceable no-smoking-indoors clauses. Source: https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/law-changes/ (read via summary).
- **3 Mar 2025 / 1 Jul 2025** - End of the 120-day rule; from 1 Jul 2025 every private rental must meet the healthy homes standards and new tenancies must comply from day one.
- **2 Jul 2025** - Insulation Part of the 2016 regulations revoked; regulations renamed Residential Tenancies (Smoke Alarms) Regulations 2016.
- **25 Sep 2025** - Residential Tenancies (Healthy Homes Standards) Amendment Regulations 2025 (SL 2025/181): partial ceiling-insulation exemption for post-2007/2008 consented dwellings with compliance documents (r 19A) and express override of overlapping Housing Improvement Regulations 1947 provisions (r 7A).
- **1 Jan 2026** - Tenancy Tribunal document-sharing change reported by realestate.co.nz (secondary source, outside scope, not verified against a primary source).
- **16 Apr 2026** - Methamphetamine regulations in force: contaminated above 15 µg/100 cm², uninhabitable above 30 µg/100 cm², mandatory detailed testing on Police/council notice or a valid screening result, prescribed decontamination and re-testing.
- **May-Jul 2026** - AMI and State (policy wordings dated 05/26) and AA Insurance (policy for periods starting on/after 1 Jul 2026) now require 6-monthly, not 3-monthly, inspections. Initio still requires 3-monthly.
- **29 Jun 2026** - New fully digital bond service (outside scope). **1 Jul 2026** - Tribunal application fee $28 to $29 (outside scope); Tiaki Wai water billing begins in the Wellington region with no change to who pays.
- **Aug 2026** - MBIE reissued the insulation guidance document.

**Proposed / pending - not law**

- **Residential Tenancies (Requiring Landlords to Provide Curtains) Amendment Bill** (member's bill, Helen White, 318-1, introduced 21 May 2026, listed as "Current"): would require the HHS to include insulated curtains, blinds or equivalents "where practicable". Parliamentary progress after introduction was not verified.
- **Residential Tenancies (Registration of Boarding House Landlords) Amendment Bill** (member's bill 183-1, introduced 17 Jul 2025, listed as "Current"): text not read.
- No Government bill amending the Residential Tenancies Act, and no amendment regulations to the HHS, smoke alarm or meth regulations later than those above, appear in a legislation.govt.nz title search on 14 Nov 2026.
