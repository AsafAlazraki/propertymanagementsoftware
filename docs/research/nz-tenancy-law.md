Other points the spec did not raise but the engine needs:

- **Electronic service is valid only to an electronic address given as an address for service** (s 136(1)(d)), and anything sent after 5 pm counts as the next working day.
- **Late fees are unenforceable** (s 32) and **rent more than 2 weeks in advance is unlawful** (s 23).
- **A second termination notice cannot be issued while the first stands** (s 51(6)).
- **Assignment bans are of no effect** (s 43A); the assignment response deadline is "reasonable", not 21 days.
- **Abandonment rent liability is 21 days** (not 28) since 30 Jan 2025.
- **Rent/bond records: 7 tax years.** Other tenancy documents: tenancy plus 12 months.
